## 1. Jasper AI

### Product Info
- **URL:** [jasper.ai](https://www.jasper.ai)
- **Tagline:** "AI purpose-built for marketing"
- **Founded:** 2021 (originally as Jarvis, rebranded to Jasper)

### Core Features
1. **Agentic AI Agents** — Autonomous agents for campaign planning, content creation, and SEO audits
2. **Brand Voice & Knowledge Base** — Learns your tone, style, and audience for consistent output across all channels
3. **50+ Content Templates** — Blog posts, social media copy, ad copy, video scripts, etc. in 30+ languages
4. **Canvas & Studio** — Collaborative workspace for long-form writing with real-time feedback
5. **SEO Optimization** — Semrush partnership for GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) and AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)
6. **AI Image Generation** — Built-in unlimited image generation for social media and landing pages
7. **Content Pipelines** — End-to-end automation connecting data, creativity, and distribution
8. **Browser Extension** — Works across Google Docs, Slack, and other web apps

### Pricing
| Plan | Monthly | Annual (per mo.) |
|------|---------|-----------------|
| Pro | $69/mo | $59/mo |
| Business | Custom pricing | Custom |

- 7-day free trial available
- Pro plan supports 2–5 users
- Business includes personalized AI, additional control, security, team training & tech support

### Target Audience
- Marketing teams (small to enterprise)
- Content marketers, product marketers, brand marketers
- Agencies managing multiple brands
- Enterprise companies in regulated industries (life sciences, retail, fintech)

### Strengths (from reviews)
- ✅ Excellent brand voice consistency across channels
- ✅ Massive template library (50+ use cases)
- ✅ Strong enterprise-grade security (9/10 security rating)
- ✅ Deep Semrush SEO integration
- ✅ Creates 3x more content, campaigns completed faster
- ✅ Multi-language support (25+ languages)
- ✅ LLM-agnostic architecture (works with multiple AI models)

### Weaknesses (from reviews)
- ❌ **Expensive** — Most-cited complaint (pricing 6/10 rating)
- ❌ Poor writing quality in some cases, content can become repetitive
- ❌ AI limitations with complex queries and narrative creativity
- ❌ Steep learning curve for new features
- ❌ Premium features locked behind higher tiers

### G2/Capterra Rating
- **G2:** ~4.7/5 (1,200+ reviews)
- **Capterra:** 4.8/5 (~500 reviews)

### Notable Customers & Social Proof
- **Customers:** Wayfair, SentinelOne, HubSpot, Morningstar
- 100,000+ customers worldwide
- 80% of users report higher social media engagement

### Unique Differentiator
**Enterprise-grade AI built specifically for marketing teams.** Unlike generic AI tools, Jasper focuses exclusively on marketing workflows with brand voice guardrails, compliance tools, and agentic AI that autonomously handles campaign tasks. The Semrush partnership for GEO/AEO optimization is unique in the space.
